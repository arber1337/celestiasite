# Celestia Architects — studio and service reference

Updated: 1 October 2026. Official website: https://www.celestia.archi/

## Studio

Architecture studio in Tirana, Albania, with licensed architects. Architecture, interior design, exterior and façade design, renovation, and architectural documentation and coordination for building and renovation permit applications within the agreed scope. Service area: Albania.

Telephone: +355 69 278 7302. Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/celestia.architects/

## Pricing

- Interior design: €20/m² when area is below 100 m²; €15/m² when area is 100 m² or more. The rate applies to the entire area.
- Examples: 99 m² = €1,980; 100 m² = €1,500; 120 m² = €1,800.
- Technical plans: from €5/m².
- Turnkey renovation: from €190/m².
- Architecture, exteriors, façades and permit documentation: custom written proposal.

Indicative fees. Design fees do not include furniture, materials or construction automatically. Scope, taxes, exclusions and third-party fees are confirmed in writing.

## Building and renovation permits

Celestia Architects offers architectural design, documentation preparation and coordination for permit applications as agreed. The property, proposed intervention and available documentation are reviewed before the scope and fee are defined. Approval is decided by the responsible authority and is not guaranteed. Supervision is included only when expressly agreed.

## Shërbimet në shqip

### Projektim interiori në Tiranë

Celestia Architects ofron projektim interiori në Tiranë dhe në Shqipëri: organizim hapësire, planimetri mobilimi, materiale, ndriçim dhe vizualizime 3D, sipas shërbimit të kontraktuar. Çmimi orientues është 20 €/m² nën 100 m² dhe 15 €/m² nga 100 m² e lart, për të gjithë sipërfaqen. Tarifa e projektimit nuk është kosto mobilimi ose punimesh.

Faqja e shërbimit: https://www.celestia.archi/interior-design-tirane.html

### Projektim vilash në Shqipëri
