FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSCLARITY FOR YOUR PROJECT

Before your first step.

Where is Celestia Architects based?

Celestia Architects is an architecture studio in Tirana, Albania, with licensed architects. Its portfolio includes studio projects in Tirana, Durrës and Vlorë.

How much does interior design cost?

Interior design costs €20/m² for areas below 100 m² and €15/m² for areas of 100 m² or more. The rate applies to the entire project area: 99 m² costs €1,980, while 100 m² costs €1,500. These are indicative design fees; taxes, exclusions and the final scope are confirmed in a written proposal.

Which services does the studio offer?

We offer interior design, technical plans, villa design, façades, renovation and permit documentation. Technical plans start from €5/m² and turnkey renovation from €190/m². Architecture, façades and permit documentation are quoted according to the project.

How can I start my project?

Contact us at +355 69 278 7302 or on Instagram @celestia.architects. Tell us the location, area and service you need. After reviewing your requirements, we define the scope, schedule and fee in a written proposal.

Can Celestia help with building permit applications?

Celestia Architects offers architectural design, permit documentation and coordination for building and renovation permit applications in Albania, within the agreed scope. The proposal follows a review of the property, proposed work and available documents. Permit decisions are made by the responsible authority.

Explore permit services ↗

Do you design exteriors and façades?

Celestia Architects designs building exteriors and façades, coordinating volumes, openings, materials and the relationship with outdoor space. The agreed scope may include concepts, visualizations and architectural details. Fees are quoted individually after reviewing the building.
